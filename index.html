Crete · Greece

Two beautiful hotels,
ten wonderful nights in Crete!

We started with seven nights at Creta Paradise in Gerani, a fantastic family-friendly all-inclusive resort with beautiful pools, a beach, its own water park, amazing food and great drinks. Our hot tub apartment was particularly spectacular and added a lovely touch of luxury!

We then moved on to Domes Aulūs, just north of Chania, for three nights of pure five-star luxury.

This spectacular ultra all-inclusive hotel really was something special, with a stunning pool, beautiful facilities, exceptional service and breathtaking views stretching out to the horizon.

Two very different hotels, both offering wonderful experiences. From family-friendly fun to luxurious relaxation, Crete really does have it all! 💙

Sardinia · Italy

Sardinia:
The Maldives Without the Jet Lag!

Sardinia has to be one of my absolute favourite places I’ve ever visited in Europe. It completely stole my heart!

Picture beautiful soft sandy beaches, sparkling crystal-clear turquoise seas, dramatic mountainous backdrops and lush greenery. Flamingos in the wetlands, cactuses growing everywhere and little geckos basking in the sunshine. Just breathtaking!

We experienced three incredible five-star hotels, travelling from the south coast to the north, each offering exceptional service, delicious food and wonderful Italian hospitality.

The beaches genuinely rival the Maldives, but without the long-haul flight or jet lag!

Sardinia feels like one of Italy’s best-kept secrets, and I am a HUGE fan. An absolutely stunning island that I cannot recommend highly enough.

If Sardinia is on your radar, I’d love to share my experiences and help you discover this incredible destination! 💚

Sharm El Sheikh · Egypt

Sharm El Sheikh:
The Perfect Winter Sunshine Escape!

We escaped to beautiful Sharm El Sheikh in March for ten nights of glorious winter sunshine, and it was absolutely perfect!

We stayed in a stunning five-star all-inclusive hotel right on the beach, enjoying fantastic food, wonderful facilities and exceptional service. Ten nights felt like just the right amount of time to completely relax and explore.

Sharm has so much more to offer than just sunshine! From the charm of Old Sharm to the more modern areas, there’s plenty to discover.

The incredible coral reefs and crystal-clear waters were a real highlight, with some of the most fantastic snorkelling experiences.

Beautiful beaches, amazing hospitality and year-round sunshine make Sharm El Sheikh a brilliant choice for a relaxing getaway with a little adventure thrown in.

A destination I’d happily recommend for some much-needed winter sunshine! 💙

MSC Virtuosa · Cruise

My First MSC Cruise Experience
on Virtuosa!

What an absolute blast! My first experience cruising with MSC was a total bargain and a week of fun, laughter and a little bit of madness with a friend!

This wasn’t so much about sightseeing, more about embracing a fun and crazy middle-aged women’s escape! 😂

We sailed in May on a Northern Europe cruise, enjoyed surprisingly gorgeous weather and made the most of the many hot tubs on board, even whilst sailing!

We had lovely little wanders around beautiful Norwegian port towns, but the real highlights were the incredible shows, parties, entertainment and, most importantly, the people we met along the way. That’s one of my favourite things about cruising!

We threw ourselves into absolutely everything and laughed our way through the week.

And sailing straight from Southampton was SO easy! No airports, no flying, just turn up and start your holiday. I declared I’d never want to set foot in an airport again… although let’s face it, that resolve didn’t last very long! 😂

MSC Virtuosa gave us a brilliant, fun-filled week and reminded me just how much I love cruising! 💙

A holiday story from Lizzie

A relaxing week in Palma Nova.

Lizzie enjoyed a relaxing stay at Globales Panama in Palma Nova. She praised the comfortable room, welcoming staff, cleanliness, warm pool and varied all-inclusive food. There was entertainment available, but no pressure to join in.

The beach was a short walk away, with clear, warm water and thoughtful accessibility features including a walkway to the sea and lifeguard support for people with mobility needs.

“I would definitely recommend this hotel and area to anyone looking for a relaxing holiday in the sun.”
A holiday story from Rachel

A Mediterranean adventure
with a perfect ending.

This summer, my husband and I went on the most amazing holiday booked with Becky at glow & go travel. We wanted an adults-only cruise and stay, and Becky found us a perfect cruise around the Mediterranean and a beautiful adults-only hotel in Paphos, where we stayed for three nights.

We had never been on a cruise before, and we absolutely loved it! Waking up in a new place to explore and sightsee during the day was perfect. Then we went back to the ship to relax and enjoy the entertainment. The food was delicious. I am gluten-free, and I always had a good choice.

Once the cruise was over, we stayed in a beautiful hotel in Paphos. The food was divine, and they were amazing with dietary requirements.

This was a big holiday for us to book. We normally go camping in the UK, and with my dietary requirements and never having booked a holiday like this, I was a bit anxious. I didn't know where to start, but Becky helped me every step of the way! Even a few days before travel, she was there to help.

“Highly recommend glow & go travel.”
Rachel
