A holiday story from Lizzie

A relaxing week in Palma Nova.

Lizzie enjoyed a relaxing stay at Globales Panama in Palma Nova. She praised the comfortable room, welcoming staff, cleanliness, warm pool and varied all-inclusive food. There was entertainment available, but no pressure to join in.

The beach was a short walk away, with clear, warm water and thoughtful accessibility features including a walkway to the sea and lifeguard support for people with mobility needs.

“I would definitely recommend this hotel and area to anyone looking for a relaxing holiday in the sun.”

Lizzie's experience, condensed from her submitted account. The photo is illustrative, not confirmed as her hotel.
